Try it on your own page in 30 seconds

Nothing to download, nothing to install.

<script src="https://neverheld-demo-production.up.railway.app/neverheld.min.js" data-auto-seal data-workers-network="none"></script>
  1. Copy the line above.
  2. Paste it just before the end of any page with a form.
  3. Reload. The page locks and shows the NEVERHELD counter.

Works with plain HTML and Next.js, tested in Chrome, Firefox and Safari's engine. For live use you host the same single 13 KB file yourself, so your page never depends on us.

Locked by your browser

Once the page is locked, its ordinary ways of sending data are refused: forms, uploads, background requests, tracking images and scripts.

Blocked by NEVERHELD

Video-call connections (WebRTC) and "get ready to connect" hints. The page does this itself, so code written to dodge it could get round it.

Not covered

Browser extensions, other tabs, anything before the lock, new windows, and a couple of browser quirks. The receipt is written by the page itself: strong evidence, not an independent audit.
