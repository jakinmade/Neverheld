What this does and does not show. After sealing, the browser's Content Security Policy refuses this page's fetch, beacon, WebSocket, form and resource requests, and NEVERHELD blocks WebRTC and network hints inside the page. Known limits: WebRTC through an iframe's own copy, a Safari preconnect, browser extensions, other tabs, and whole-tab navigation. The proof is written by the page itself; it is evidence, not a signed record of the session or an independent audit. Read the full limits.
